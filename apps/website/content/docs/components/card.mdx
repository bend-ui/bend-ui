# Surface and Card

Use Surface for a contextual background and Card to group related content within it. The [working workspace](/docs/components/overlays-navigation) nests both and opens portalled overlays from the raised surface.

```tsx
<SurfaceProvider>
  <Surface contextual surfaceLevel="page">
    <Card contextual>
      <Card.Header>
        <Card.Title>Workspace</Card.Title>
      </Card.Header>
      <Card.Body>Project details</Card.Body>
    </Card>
  </Surface>
</SurfaceProvider>
```

The `surface` and `card` recipes are independently customizable. Context is opt-in on Surface and Card and caps at overlay. Card offers Root, Header, Title, Description, Body, Footer and Section. Explicit consumer styles retain precedence.
